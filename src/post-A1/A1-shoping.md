Practise your reading with this simple text. It uses easy words and short sentences.

---

Today is Saturday. Sofia goes shopping. She likes shopping with her mother.

First, they go to the market. Sofia buys apples and bananas. Her mother buys tomatoes and onions. The market is big and noisy.

Next, they go to a clothes shop. Sofia wants a new dress. She sees a blue dress. It is beautiful. Sofia tries the dress. It fits well. Her mother buys the dress for her.

After that, they are hungry. They go to a small restaurant. Sofia eats rice and chicken. Her mother eats fish and salad. They drink orange juice.

In the afternoon, they go home. Sofia is happy. She has a new dress and a full stomach.

"Thank you, Mom," says Sofia.

"You're welcome," says her mother. "See you next Saturday!"

---

## Vocabulary Notes

- *shopping* — compras
- *market* — mercado
- *clothes shop* — loja de roupa
- *dress* — vestido
- *hungry* — com fome
- *stomach* — estômago

## Comprehension Questions

1. What day is it in the story?
2. What does Sofia buy at the market?
3. What colour is the dress?
4. Where do they eat lunch?
5. What does Sofia drink?
