Practise your reading with this simple text. It uses easy words and short sentences.

---

My name is Ana. I am 25 years old. I live in Luanda with my family.

Every day, I wake up at 6:30 AM. I have breakfast with my mother and my sister. We eat bread and drink coffee.

I go to work by bus. My job is in a school. I am a teacher. I teach children English.

At 12:00, I have lunch. I eat rice and chicken. Sometimes I eat fish.

After work, I go home. I watch TV or read a book. I like books about animals.

At night, I have dinner with my family. We talk about our day. Then I go to bed at 10:00 PM.

I like my life. It is simple and happy.

---

## Vocabulary Notes

- *wake up* — acordar
- *breakfast* — pequeno-almoço
- *teacher* — professor(a)
- *lunch* — almoço
- *dinner* — jantar

## Comprehension Questions

1. What time does Ana wake up?
2. What is her job?
3. What does she eat for lunch?
4. What does she do after work?
