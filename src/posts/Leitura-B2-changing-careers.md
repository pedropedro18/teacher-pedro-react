After spending almost ten years working as an accountant, Sofia made a decision that surprised everyone around her: she quit her stable job to pursue a career in graphic design. Many people questioned her choice, especially given the financial security she was giving up, but Sofia had been feeling increasingly unfulfilled for quite some time.

It all started during the pandemic, when she found herself with more free time than usual. Instead of watching television or scrolling through social media, she began taking online courses in design software. What began as a simple hobby gradually turned into a genuine passion. She would spend hours experimenting with colours, typography, and layouts, often losing track of time completely.

Convincing her family wasn't easy. Her parents, who had always valued stability above all else, were understandably concerned about the risks involved in switching careers at the age of thirty-two. "Are you sure this is the right decision?" her mother asked repeatedly. Sofia understood their worries, but she had already made up her mind.

She spent the following year building a portfolio, taking on freelance projects whenever possible, and networking with professionals in the design industry. It wasn't always smooth sailing — there were moments of self-doubt, financial uncertainty, and long nights questioning whether she had made the right choice. Nevertheless, she persisted.

Eighteen months later, Sofia landed a position at a mid-sized design agency. Although her salary was initially lower than what she had earned as an accountant, she felt something she hadn't experienced in years: genuine enthusiasm for her work. Looking back, she has no regrets about the path she chose, even considering all the challenges she faced along the way.

---

## Vocabulary Notes

- *unfulfilled* — insatisfeito, sem realização pessoal
- *freelance* — trabalho independente/autónomo
- *portfolio* — portefólio (conjunto de trabalhos)
- *networking* — criação de contactos profissionais
- *smooth sailing* — expressão idiomática: "andar tudo bem", sem dificuldades
- *self-doubt* — dúvida sobre si próprio, insegurança

## Comprehension Questions

1. What was Sofia's original career, and what did she change it to?
2. What triggered her interest in graphic design?
3. How did her parents react to her decision, and why?
4. What challenges did Sofia face during her transition?
5. Was Sofia's new salary higher or lower than her previous one? Did this matter to her in the end?
