Practise your reading with this text. It has slightly longer sentences than A1, but the vocabulary is still simple.

---

It's Friday afternoon, and Carlos is excited. Tomorrow is Saturday, and he doesn't have to work. He is planning a fun weekend with his friends.

On Saturday morning, Carlos is going to meet his friend Pedro at the market. They want to buy fresh fruit and vegetables for a barbecue. Carlos loves cooking, so he is going to prepare chicken and grilled corn.

In the afternoon, more friends are coming to Carlos's house. They are going to play music, eat together, and talk for hours. Carlos's sister, Rita, is bringing a cake she made herself.

On Sunday, Carlos wants to relax. He is going to sleep late and then go for a walk by the beach. He enjoys listening to the sound of the waves. In the evening, he is going to call his parents, who live in another city, and tell them about his weekend.

Carlos always says that weekends are important. They give him energy for the next week of work.

---

## Vocabulary Notes

- *barbecue* — churrasco
- *grilled corn* — milho grelhado
- *relax* — relaxar
- *waves* — ondas
- *energy* — energia

## Comprehension Questions

1. What is Carlos planning to do on Saturday morning?
2. Who is bringing a cake?
3. What does Carlos want to do on Sunday morning?
4. Why does Carlos think weekends are important?
